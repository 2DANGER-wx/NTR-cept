Engineering notes

Frame mass follows tube length and hollow steel cross-section. Armor mass follows plate area and thickness. Wind loading affects movement, lift, traction, and overturn risk.

Assumptions / save controls

Air density: 1.225 kg/m³. Steel density: 7,850 kg/m³. Frame tubes: 70 mm outside diameter, 5 mm wall thickness. Armor areas scale with vehicle dimensions.

Stock curb masses, frontal areas, and drag coefficients are representative estimates. Crosswind and lift coefficients, soil holding, equipment strength, and damage are gameplay assumptions. A side-view drawing cannot determine actual 3D aerodynamics.

Only installed frames are saved. Imported images and progress stay in this browser when local storage is available.
